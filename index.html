rclone-chat-monitor

rclone-chat-monitor is a private, personal-use utility for archiving chat screenshot captures and synchronizing those archives to the owner's Google Drive account.

The application is not offered as a public service, does not sell user data, and does not use Google Drive data for advertising.

Privacy PolicyTerms of Service

Google Drive access

Google Drive access is used only to create and manage archive files generated by this application after explicit authorization by the account owner.

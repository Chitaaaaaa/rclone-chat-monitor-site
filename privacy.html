Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Purpose

rclone-chat-monitor is a personal-use utility that archives chat screenshot captures and synchronizes archive files to the owner's Google Drive account.

Google Drive data

The application accesses Google Drive only after explicit authorization by the Google account owner. Drive access is used solely to create, upload, manage, and retain archive files produced by this application.

Data collection and sharing

The application is not operated as a public data service. It does not sell personal information, does not share Google Drive data with advertisers, and does not use Google Drive data for advertising or profiling.

Storage

Archive files are stored in the Google Drive account authorized by the owner. Temporary local copies may exist on the owner's infrastructure while files are being processed or uploaded.

Revoking access

The account owner may revoke the application's Google account access at any time through Google Account security settings. Revoking access stops future Drive synchronization.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the GitHub repository issue tracker.
